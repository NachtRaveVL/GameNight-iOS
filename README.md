# GameNight-iOS

GameNight: Beat decision paralysis and find a game to play tonight!

GameNight-iOS v0.0.1

## About

With all the games that are available to play in this day and age, it can become very difficult to choose what to play next. GameNight is our vision for fighting against decision paralysis and organizing your gaming library. Whenever you play modern or retro, this App is all about trying to help gamers decide on what to play next. Discover games you never even knew about along with marking the ones you have beat, take back your time and dive into something that captures your attention.
